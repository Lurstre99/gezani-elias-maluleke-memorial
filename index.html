Obituary

Mufi Gezani Elias Maluleke u beburiwe etikweni ra Tshimbupfhe hi lembe ra 1947. Mina Gezani Elias Maluleke a niri n’wana wa vunharhu eka Nxalati n’waDumela na Jan Masungwini.

Va sungurile tidyondzo ta vona exikolweni xa Pfukani kona etikweni ra Tshimbupfhe laha vanga dyondza kufika eka ntanga nharhu. Va xambirile hi lembe ra 1964 vaya eTzaneen va tirhanyana lembe mambiri, va suka hi lembe ra 1967 vaya ePhalaborwa, laha vanga tirhanyana ku fika eka lembe ya nkombo.

Ku suka kona va xambirile vaya eJoni hi lembe ra 1975 laha vanga fika vakuma nkarhi eJansmuts Airport (eka railway), laha vanga tirha kona ku fika hi lembe ra 2004, laha vanga khomiwa hi mavabyi ya matihlo. Va hume phexeni hi 2005.

Mufi u siya nsati na vana va ntlhanu kunga Dennis, Mercy, Rirombiso, Laster na Nyiko na vatukulu va ntlhanu.

Etlela hi kurhula Jaha ra ka Guyu, wa Maxakadzi wa Malenga, wa Matoto ya tihuku ya lema rihlelo.
